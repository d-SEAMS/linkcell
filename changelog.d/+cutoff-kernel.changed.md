The cutoff tile forms `|r_q|^2 - 2 r_q . p' + |p'|^2` from positions relative to the bin corner, with three fused multiply-adds per lane. A lane within a rounding bound of the cutoff squared is decided by the direct formula, so the rows do not change; `dist2` agrees with the direct value to within that bound (about 2e-10 Å² on an 18 Å cube). One thread writes a full list straight from the tile. The bins are a fold, a per-bin count, a scan, and a scatter into slot columns, and a search with several threads runs on a pool thread.
