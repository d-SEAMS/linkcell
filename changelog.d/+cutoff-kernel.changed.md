The cutoff tile forms `|r_q|^2 - 2 r_q . p' + |p'|^2` from positions relative to the bin corner, with three fused multiply-adds per lane. A lane within a rounding bound of the cutoff squared is decided by the direct formula, so the rows do not change; `dist2` agrees with the direct value to within that bound (about 2e-10 Å² on an 18 Å cube). One thread writes a full list straight from the tile. The bins are a fold, a per-bin count, a scan, and a scatter into slot columns; the fold takes eight points at a time on AVX-512 and gives the same bits as `Cell::fractional` and `Cell::cartesian`, for the k-nearest mesh as well. A walk that splits across threads (from ten thousand expected pairs) reserves its rows on the caller's thread, then searches and writes in one pool entry. On this host a periodic 18 Å cube, 4 Å cutoff, and 4096 atoms takes 1.59 ms on one thread and 0.33 ms on eight from Rust, against 2.25 and 0.80 ms for 0.3.8 over fifteen interleaved rounds; in another run 0.3.8 took 0.46 ms on eight threads and this build 0.29 ms. Python takes 1.33 and 0.31 ms against 5.68 and 4.44 ms, C 1.71 and 0.26 ms against 4.32 and 3.82 ms, and C++ 2.91 and 2.07 ms against 9.62 and 8.00 ms. vesin 0.6.2's cell list takes 32 ms and 6 to 9 ms.
