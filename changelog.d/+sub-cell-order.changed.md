The cutoff bins keep their occupants in a Morton order of two or four sub-cells per axis, about one atom each, so a vector of eight targets is a compact block of its bin whatever order the caller's atoms come in. A split walk folds a block of atoms per thread into its own staging area grouped by bin, then each thread sorts and writes its own run of bins, so no cache line is written by two threads and the order is one thread's. The 4096-atom probe can shuffle its atoms (`PAIRS_SHUFFLE`, and `KNN_SCALE_SHUFFLE` for the k-nearest probe); shuffled, the cutoff call takes 1.67 ms on one thread and 0.34 ms on eight, against 2.21 and 0.44 ms in atom order before and 2.93 and 0.82 ms for 0.3.8.
