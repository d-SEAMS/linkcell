`pairs_within` buffers cutoff hits and, with more than one thread and at least 512 atoms, writes disjoint ranges of one pair buffer. A full list still has both shifts. On Linux that buffer stays on the heap so a repeated call does not fault those pages again. On this host a periodic 18 Å cube, 4 Å cutoff, and 4096 atoms is 0.7 ms once the buffer is warm on 8 threads, and the first call is about 3 ms.
