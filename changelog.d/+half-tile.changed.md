A half list on one thread (`pairs_within` and `pairs_within_columns` with `half` set, and Python's `pairs_within(half=True)`) is written straight from the AVX-512 tile, one row per hit on the side `keep_half` keeps, instead of buffering hits and writing each row with a scalar branch. The rows, their order, and their bits match the eight-thread buffered writer. On this host the periodic 18 Å cube at a 4 Å cutoff takes 0.82 ms at 4096 atoms (was 1.33 ms; 0.3.8 1.88 ms), 0.0185 ms at 256 atoms (was 0.0235; 0.3.8 0.037), and 0.20 ms at 1536 atoms (was 0.24; 0.3.8 0.38). The buffered writers (C's `lc_pairs_within` and `lc_pairs_within_rows` callers on several threads, Python and Rust on several threads) write half lists eight hits at a time on AVX-512 as well: C's one-thread half list at 4096 atoms takes 1.14 ms (was 1.51; 0.3.8 3.04 ms).
