`knearest`, `knearest_into`, `knearest_into_d2`, `knearest_into_many`, `knearest_csr` and `knearest_brute` return `Error::NonFinite` for a NaN or infinite coordinate (masked or not) or cell origin, as `pairs_within_csr` does, instead of searching with clamped bins and returning wrong neighbours. The check is one pass over the coordinates before any search; a batch is checked whole before its first frame, and the output rows are cleared to `-1` and `NaN`. `lc_knearest`, `lc_knearest_d2`, `lc_knearest_many` and `lc_knearest_csr` return nonzero with `a coordinate is not finite` in `lc_last_error()`, the C++ wrappers throw `linkcell::Error`, and Python `knearest` raises `RuntimeError` for host tensors. A NaN or infinite lattice entry stays `Error::BadBox` (Python `ValueError`).
