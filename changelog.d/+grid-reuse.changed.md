A repeated cutoff search reuses its grid. The slot columns, bin offsets, corners, fold, and counts come back from the last grid without zeroing, and the bin stencil (target bins, shifts, and corner offsets) is kept for the same box, bin counts, reach, and cutoff. A walk that splits across threads builds its bins on every worker from 1024 atoms, which also wakes the workers before the search. A 256-atom call in an 18 Å cube drops from 0.032 to 0.020 ms, and the 4096-atom call on eight threads from 0.38 to 0.33 ms.
