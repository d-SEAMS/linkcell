The cutoff writers prefetch the line 52 rows past each store once their output passes 3 MB a thread: the one-thread tile for rows and columns, the buffered AVX-512 writers, and the scalar row, half-list, column, and C writers. A store whose line was out in L3 waited at the head of the store buffer, and once the buffer filled the tile stalled behind it, so the walk and the row stream took turns. On this host the periodic 18 Å cube at a 4 Å cutoff with 4096 atoms drops from 1.47 to 1.15 to 1.21 ms on one thread; from 1536 atoms the gain is 10 to 16%, on two and four threads 2 to 4%, and on eight threads none. 256 and 1024 atoms stay under the threshold and are unchanged. The threshold is `tune` knob 4, and `scripts/tune-pairs.py` searches it.
