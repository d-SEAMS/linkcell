The `tune` feature adds autotuner hooks, `lc_tune_set` for the cutoff walk's thresholds and paths and `lc_tune_pairs` for a timed call with a checksum of its rows; the shipped header declares neither. `scripts/tune-pairs.py` searches them with Kernel Tuner and checks every configuration's rows against the default. On this host the defaults sit within timing noise of the best of 1080 configurations on eight threads and 18 on one thread for the 4096-atom cube.
