A `sanitize=thread` build links every consumer of `linkcell_dep` and `linkcell_gpu_dep`, and the pkg-config `Libs`, with `-fsanitize=thread`. The sanitized library leaves the `__tsan_*` symbols to the final link, so the examples and any executable or library that did not pass `-fsanitize=thread` itself failed to link or to load. Configure stops when the C compiler cannot link with the flag.
