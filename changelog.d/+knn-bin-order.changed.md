The k-nearest walk visits sources in bin order and reads positions from slot-ordered copies, and each source writes its own output row, so a neighbour's shell is still in cache whatever order the caller's points come in. Sources go in blocks of bins that share one gathered candidate list; each source forms its distances eight at a time and pushes into its heap only the points under a bound on its k-th nearest, and a source the gathered bins do not settle resumes the shell walk, so every row and `dist2` is the shell walk's. The threaded mesh build forms its offsets and cursors in parallel over the bins. For the 262144-point cube and k = 4, one thread takes 37 ms and eight take 6.3 ms, against 66 and 11 ms for 0.3.8; shuffled, 64 and 15.6 ms against 251 and 40 ms; the tilted cell 41 and 6.8 ms against 72 and 11.8 ms.
