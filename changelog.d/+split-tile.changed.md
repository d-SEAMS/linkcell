On several threads, a full list of `Pair` rows on AVX-512 whose rows pass 3 MB a thread is written from the tile kernel: each thread counts its range of bins' rows, then writes them at their offset, with the registers that would reach the next thread's rows stored under a mask, instead of buffering every hit and writing the rows in a second pass. Rows, their order, and their bits are unchanged. On this host the periodic 18 Å cube at a 4 Å cutoff with 4096 atoms takes 0.278 ms on eight threads (was 0.321 ms) and 0.938 ms on two (was 1.006 ms); smaller lists keep the buffered path.
