The cutoff bins are built from fold columns with the sort key formed in registers, and the slot columns are filled in slot order from atom numbers sorted by key; the bins for 4096 atoms take 0.038 ms on one thread, against 0.061 ms. When the bins hold four runs of eight on average, each bin also records the bounding boxes of its runs of eight targets and four sources, and the tile skips a target run whose box is a cutoff (plus twice the rounding bound) from the source run's, eight target runs per vector test, as in GROMACS's cluster pairs. The rows, their order, and their `dist2` bits do not change. For the 4096-atom cube the tile tests 124 thousand source-vectors instead of 444 thousand, and the one-thread call drops from 1.49 to 1.42 ms.
